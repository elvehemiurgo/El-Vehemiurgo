## Identificación

- **Nombre real**: Kurt Steven Angle.
- **Origen**: Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. 9 dic 1968.
- **Edad**: 57 años (mayo 2026). Mostly retired.
- **Background**: NCAA Division I Heavyweight Wrestling
  Champion 1990, **Olympic Gold Medal Heavyweight Wrestling
  Atlanta 1996** ("with a broken freakin' neck").
- **Status actual**: WWE Hall of Famer 2017, ocasional
  appearances.
- **Finisher signature**: ***Ankle Lock***, ***Angle Slam***
  (Olympic Slam).
- **Apodo**: *Olympic Hero / Wrestling Machine / You Suck!*
