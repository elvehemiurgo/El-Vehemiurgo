# Bret 'The Hitman' Hart

> **The Hitman — Best There Is, Best There Was, Best There
> Ever Will Be**. Hart Foundation patriarch contemporary +
> WWF Champion 5× + WCW Champion 2× + Montreal Screwjob
> victim + víctima de injury Goldberg WCW 1999. Pieza de
> sec. 5 del notebook *"Bret Hart deep cuts — carny
> tradition"*.
>
> **Pillar of Wrestling #8 del Vehemiurgo** — panteón
> ([`../topics/heroes-fundamentales-vehemiurgia.md`](../topics/heroes-fundamentales-vehemiurgia.md)),
> rama worker de oficio (declarado 2026-05-26, #8).

## Identificación

- **Nombre real**: Bret Sergeant Hart.
- **Origen**: Calgary, Alberta, Canada. 2 jul 1957.
- **Edad**: 68 años (mayo 2026). Retirado.
- **Familia wrestling**: Stu Hart (padre, Stampede Wrestling
  founder + dungeon trainer). 12 hermanos incluidos Owen †1999,
  Bruce, Smith, Keith, Wayne, Dean †1990, Ross, Diana
  (married Davey Boy Smith).
- **Finisher signature**: ***Sharpshooter*** (scorpion
  deathlock variant adoptado de Pat Patterson via Riki Choshu).
- **Apodo**: *The Hitman*, *Best There Is, Best There Was,
  Best There Ever Will Be*.

## Trayectoria condensada

- **Stampede Wrestling** 1976-1984 (territorial Hart family
  promotion).
- **WWF 1984-1997**: Hart Foundation tag (con Jim Neidhart),
  Intercontinental Champion múltiples runs, **WWF Champion
  5×**, King of the Ring 1993 winner, **WrestleMania XII Iron
  Man vs HBK** (60 min), **Survivor Series 1997 Montreal
  Screwjob** (Vince+HBK conspiracy, Bret betrayed) — **moment
  más editorialmente significativo del wrestling moderno**.
- **WCW 1997-2000**: WCW Champion 2×, post-Screwjob disillusioned
  era. **Goldberg injury dic 1999** (botched mule kick →
  permanent concussion damage, forced retirement).
- **Retirement 2000-2010** post-stroke 2002 + brain damage
  recovery.
- **WWE return 2010** brief (Raw GM era + WrestleMania
  XXVI vs Vince McMahon).
- **WWE Hall of Fame 2006**.

## Notas Vehemiurgia (sec. 5 notebook)

> cita 4 deep cuts Bret: vs Owen Raw pre-WMXI, vs The Mountie

más allá del PPV canónico** — Raw tapings, live events,
defenses territoriales, Coliseum Video Exclusives. Pieza
editorial sobre **canales distribution carny pre-internet**.

## Linaje editorial

**Bret encarna la tradición Hart family Stampede + Calgary
catch + carny old-school**. Linaje del **technical babyface
canadiense puro** + **ring general modern era**. Pieza
editorial obligatoria sobre **el booker que trabajó dentro
del kayfabe + el victim del booker que rompió kayfabe
(Vince Montreal Screwjob)**.
