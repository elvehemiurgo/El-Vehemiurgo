# The Rock (Dwayne Johnson)

> **People's Champion + Attitude Era pillar + Hollywood
> superstar transcontextual**. Carrera dual: WWE 1996-2004
> primer run + Hollywood 2002+ paralelo + WWE returns 2011+
> selectivos + **TKO Board of Directors 2024+** (cambio
> editorial corporate). Pieza centerpiece **Wrestling
> Entertainment Class candidato canónico** (Austin/Rock IYH
> 1997).

## Identificación

- **Nombre real**: Dwayne Douglas Johnson.
- **Origen**: Hayward, CA → raised Honolulu/Bethlehem PA.
  2 may 1972.
- **Edad**: 53 años (mayo 2026).
- **Familia wrestling royalty**: Anoaʻi family via mother
  (Ata Maivia Johnson, hija de Peter Maivia). Padre Rocky
  Johnson (WWF Tag Team Champion 1980s). **Cross-cultural
  Samoan-Black wrestling royalty**.
- **Status actual**: TKO Board of Directors miembro 2024+ +
  ocasional WWE on-screen + Hollywood blockbuster star.
- **Finisher signature**: ***Rock Bottom*** (uranage), ***People's
  Elbow*** (theatrical elbow drop signature), ***Sharpshooter***
  (homenaje Hart family).
- **Apodo**: *The Rock*, *The People's Champion*, *The Brahma
  Bull*, *The Great One*.

## Trayectoria condensada

- **WWF debut Survivor Series 1996** como Rocky Maivia (face).
- **Heel turn 1997** Nation of Domination — character work
  reset que define la era.
- **Cluster Vehemiurgia Wrestling Entertainment Class anchor**:
  - **Austin vs The Rock WWF In Your House 19 7 dic 1997** —
    candidato canónico Wrestling Entertainment Class
    ([`../matches/1997-12-07-steve-austin-vs-the-rock-wwf-in-your-house.md`](../matches/1997-12-07-steve-austin-vs-the-rock-wwf-in-your-house.md)).
- **Multiple WWF/E Champion** + **WrestleMania main eventer
  XV (1999), XVII vs Austin (2001), XVIII vs Hogan (2002),
  XIX vs Austin (2003)**.
- **Brief WWF leave 2002-2004** post-WMXIX Austin retirement
  match.
- **Hollywood era 2002+ exclusivo** — Scorpion King + Walking
  Tall + Mummy Returns → Fast & Furious franchise + Jumanji
  reboot + multiple blockbusters.
- **WWE returns selectivos** 2011-2013 (vs Cena WMXXVIII +
  XXIX), 2016 (Royal Rumble +1 elimination record).
- **2024+ TKO Board of Directors** + WrestleMania XL eve
  storyline (Rock vs Cody buildup → switched to Roman main
  event).
- **2025-2026 cluster cross-link**: Rock continúa como
  occasional on-screen TKO presence + meta-storyline
  *play wrestlers / corporate face* implicit (board member +
  Hollywood persona).

## Notas Vehemiurgia

- **Wrestling Entertainment Class anchor canónico**: Austin
  vs The Rock 1997 IYH como pieza editorial central de la
  clase Wrestling Entertainment.
- **Cross-cultural carny puro**: Anoaʻi family via mother +
  Black father lineage = arquetipo cross-cultural americano
  modern era. **Pieza editorial fértil sobre talent
  multi-heritage**.
- **Hollywood / WWE bifurcation**: pieza editorial sobre
  **talent que trasciende el wrestling al mainstream
  Hollywood** — modelo que después varios talents intentan
  (Cena, John Bautista, Roman Reigns, Jacob Fatu vía
  Bloodline) sin llegar al Rock-level.
- **TKO Board membre 2024+**: cambio editorial complicación
  — Rock como **corporate side de la doctrina meta-storyline**
  *real wrestlers vs play wrestlers* implicit.
