# Chris Jericho

> **Y2J — talent reinvención permanente**. Carrera 35+ años
> a través de Stampede + México + Japan + ECW + WCW + WWF/WWE
> + AEW founding. Pieza central del cluster **WWE Raw 2004-2005
> Christian/Jericho era**. Creador del **Money in the Bank
> ladder match concept** (WrestleMania 21 inaugural 2005).

## Identificación

- **Nombre real**: Christopher Keith Irvine.
- **Origen**: Manhasset, NY USA / Winnipeg, Manitoba Canada
  (raised). 9 nov 1970.
- **Edad activo**: 55 años (mayo 2026).
- **Status actual**: AEW talent + podcast host + multiple
  ventures.
- **Finisher signature**: ***Walls of Jericho*** (Boston crab
  variant), ***Codebreaker*** (jumping double knees), ***Liontamer***
  (Walls original más severo), ***Judas Effect***.
- **Apodo**: *Y2J*, *The Living Legend*, *Le Champion*, *The
  Demo God*.

## Trayectoria condensada

- **Stampede + indies 1990-1996** (Hart Foundation Dungeon
  trained).
- **México (Promo Azteca, CMLL) + Japan (WAR, NJPW) 1990s**.
- **ECW 1996 brief** + cruiserweight tradition.
- **WCW 1996-1999**: Cruiserweight Champion, peak heel
  comedy character work, eventual WCW departure por
  frustración con Hogan/nWo politics.
- **WWF debut 9 ago 1999** — *"Y2J countdown"* segment con
  The Rock.
- **WWE Undisputed Champion** 2001 (primer en unificar WWF
  + WCW Titles vs Steve Austin + The Rock same night
  Vengeance 2001).
- **Cluster Raw 2004-2005** central — feud Christian-Trish
  triangle peak (WMXX + Backlash 2004 + steel cage 10 may
  2004 + Unforgiven 2004 + triple threat 4 abr 2005). Dossier
  dedicado:
  [`../../dossiers/draft-wwe-raw-2004-2005-christian-jericho-era.md`](../../dossiers/draft-wwe-raw-2004-2005-christian-jericho-era.md).
- **Money in the Bank concept inventor** — propuso a Vince
  el formato MITB ladder, debutó WrestleMania 21 2005.
- **Multiple WWE returns** 2007, 2012, 2016 (List of Jericho
  era), heel + face cycles.
- **AEW founding 2019** — primer AEW World Champion (Double
  or Nothing 2019). Le Champion + Inner Circle + Jericho
  Appreciation Society factions.
- **Talk Is Jericho podcast** (2013+) — uno de los podcasts
  wrestling más influentes contemporary, retrospectivas
  sostenidas.
