# MJF (Maxwell Jacob Friedman)

> **Modern heel old-school** — character work carny puro
> villain "wrestling's biggest superstar of tomorrow" gimmick.
> AEW founding generation talent + AEW World Champion múltiples
> runs + feud Hangman Adam Page peak 2026 cluster.

## Identificación

- **Nombre real**: Maxwell Jacob Friedman.
- **Origen**: Plainview, New York. 15 mar 1996.
- **Edad activo**: 30 años (mayo 2026).
- **Status actual**: AEW main eventer, current run heel reset
  2026.
- **Finisher signature**: ***Heatseeker*** (piledriver), ***Salt
  of the Earth*** (cross-armbreaker), ***Diamond Ring punch***
  (loaded ring strike).
- **Apodo**: *Wrestling's Biggest Superstar of Tomorrow*,
  *The Devil*, *Better Than You and You Know It*.
